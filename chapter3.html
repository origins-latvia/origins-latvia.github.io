- 1945 -

View full size

04 May -- The whole column, including Germans, moves on. They get us all together in a village near there and take us further in trucks - about 26km. We camp in a field again together with the Germans. We put up a tent and fall into a deep sleep. Today we've marched 10km. Mölln Camp.

05 May -- We lay around in the tents - terribly bad weather. Today we got our first POW provisions: 8 biscuits and 60gm corned beef.

06 May -- We lie around all day and receive no food - except for green potatoes* toward night time.
* This episode was one of the very few things Dad ever mentioned with bitterness whenever there was criticism of the way in which allied POWs were treated.

07 May -- At night we get ⅓L rice soup and 100gm preserves. During the day nothing at all to eat. In the evening "Peace in all of Europe" is announced over the radio.

08 May -- In the morning 200gm bread. Peace is in effect.

09 May -- Peace was actually declared on 9 May.

13 May -- It's Sunday. The cherry trees are in full bloom. The grass has grown quite high.

25. mai. Pirmie vasaras svētki pirms pusdienas nodzīvoju par istabu un no Repšas J nopirku sporta kreklu. Pēc pusdienas iešu pastaigāties kopā Zani un Emmu. Un vakarā uz Knapzali apskatīt bali. Laiks jauks bet man ir tik drūmi --

dd mmm -- .... what to do. It's like everything inside me is boiling.

25 May -- First [day of] Vasarsvētki [Whitsuntide / Pentecost]. Before lunchtime I pass the time in my room and I buy a sports shirt from J. Repšis. After lunch I'm going to go for a walk with Zana and Emma. In the evening to Knapzali to have a look at the dancing. The weather is pleasant but I'm very down --

25 May, 1947 was the last entry in this diary .... the journey is continued in the small diary starting 29 Sept, 1948.

DP camps in Dinkelsbühl, Bavaria 1945-1949:

5 May 1945: In a report of Headquarters Military Government - US-Army on Displaced Persons (Kreis DINKELSBUEH) TOTAL 6081 persons of different nationalities were mentioned.

Soon this high number of people was reduced by transporting a lot of them to other DP camps in the neighborhood in Bavaria.

So in a continued report on DPs in centers in Dinkelsbuehl and immediate vicinity, TOTAL 627 persons were registered.

The different places of the DP camps in Dinkelsbuehl:e

Hotels:
Dutsches Haus (German House)
Goldene Rose (Golden Rose)
Weisses Ross (White Horse): Here were the Lettish DPs.

Guesthouses:
Dinkelbauer (Spelt Farmer)
Brauner Hirsch (Brown Deer)
Kornschranne (Grain House)

Schools: - Former Oberrealschule (Secondary School)
Today Vocational School, Noerdlinger Strasse 22
- Former Fliegerschule ( Flying School) - Then Hats-Factory Peschel -
Today City's Department for Elecricity, Rudolf-Schmidt-Strasse 7

Other housings:
- Bahnhofsrestauration - (Im Knabsaal), Luitpoldstrasse 19:
Here were accommodated the Lithuanian DPs.
- Private house, former brush factory: Noerdlinger Strasse 52:
Here lived the Ukrainian DPs.
